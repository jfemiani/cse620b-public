Can satellite indices track leaf area at Harvard Forest?

In this 25–30 minute activity, compare multispectral and radar images at Prospect Hill, Massachusetts, with field measurements of leaf area index (LAI).

Open the Harvard Forest lab in Google Colab or download the notebook.

In class

  1. Download the aligned Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1 chips and the Harvard Forest field measurements. Inspect the RGB image and the pixels kept for analysis.
  2. Compute optical and radar indices. Compare their correlations with field LAI across the 2022–2023 site dates.
  3. Fit a line for one optical and one radar predictor. Test both on 2024 dates and compare their errors against a training-mean baseline.
  4. Explain what the observed relationships tell you about seasonal change at this site, and what they do not tell you about other forests.

The radar time series has VV/VH polarization. The corresponding ratio and difference use those channels, not the HH/HV pair used in some lecture examples. The notebook explains the distinction.

The notebook links to the Harvard Forest field dataset and worked tutorials for optical NDVI and terrain-corrected radar.
