# Forestry and vegetation: sources and image notes

The published `forestry-and-vegetation.pdf` was rebuilt on November 16, 2026 from the instructor's current `IRS-Chapter-18-Slides.md`: the 49-slide "Remote Sensing Applications: Forestry" deck. It preserves that document's content and figures. The original PDF and PowerPoint remain in the lesson's `pdf` folder.

The adjacent `forestry-and-vegetation.md` is an earlier alternative draft, not the source of the current PDF. Its five diagrams in `images/` are retained for the lecture notes. They are schematic illustrations; the spectrum and saturation curve are not observations or fitted datasets. The lidar returns are illustrative, not real point-cloud data.
