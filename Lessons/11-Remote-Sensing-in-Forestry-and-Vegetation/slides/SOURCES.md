# Forestry and vegetation: sources and image notes

The lecture was developed from the existing IRS Chapter 18 forestry slides in this course and revised to emphasize interpretation, uncertainty, and current applications. The original PDF and PowerPoint remain in the lesson's `pdf` folder.

All five diagrams in `images/` were created for this lecture. They are schematic illustrations; the spectrum and saturation curve are not observations or fitted datasets. The lidar returns are illustrative, not real point-cloud data.
